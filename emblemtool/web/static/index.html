Mode

The editor only checks once per session. After you open your emblem editor the first time, the game caches what it loaded and won't check again, even if you select a different emblem here. To load a different one, restart the game, or switch to Zombies and back to Multiplayer, before opening the editor again.

Locked shapes won't work. If a captured emblem uses a shape, rank, or weapon icon your own account hasn't unlocked, the game may fail to load or save it. That's Black Ops II enforcing it, not something this tool can get around.

Your captured emblems

Click one to select it. Once you switch to Show and open your own emblem editor, this is the emblem that loads there, ready to save as your own. The × in a card's corner deletes it.

No emblems captured yet. Switch to Capture mode, then open a player's profile on your PS5.

Troubleshooting

Something not working? Turn this on, try whatever failed again, then click Open logs folder and send the developer the newest file there, for example by attaching it to a GitHub issue. The log records what the toolkit did, every error, and which servers your PS5 contacted. It never includes your emblems, login details, or other players' IDs.
